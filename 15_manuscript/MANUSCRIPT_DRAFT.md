# PerturbBridge: what the ENCODE RNA-binding-protein knockdown compendium can and cannot teach a model about unseen regulators

Draft v0.1, 2026-11-09. Sections marked [PENDING] are filled from 14_inverse/ outputs when the jobs finish. Numbers are taken from STATUS.md entries dated 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-09 and the files named in brackets.

## Abstract (draft)

Predicting how cells respond to the loss of a regulator they have never been perturbed for is the central promise of perturbation models. We asked whether this is achievable at the splicing layer using the largest public resource, the ENCODE RNA-binding protein (RBP) knockdown and eCLIP compendium (876 knockdown RNA-seq experiments, 478 RBPs, two cell lines). We rebuilt the splicing readout at junction level (rMATS-turbo on 876 knockdown/control pairs; 546,917 coordinate-keyed events) and found two artefacts that inflate apparent accuracy: a shared-control artefact (knockdowns compared to the same control inherit its noise; r 0.49–0.51 between unrelated RBPs) and a control regression-to-the-mean artefact (events called significant are enriched for runs whose control PSI deviates from the event's typical value; a zero-parameter rule predicts the direction of "significant" events with 0.83/0.79 accuracy). After removing both, the direction of knockdown-induced splicing changes for an unseen RBP is predictable at 0.51–0.52 across all RBPs (ceiling from independent experiments of the same RBP: 0.56–0.57) and at 0.68–0.69 for the ~30 RBPs whose own knockdown effects reproduce (shuffled-identity control 0.46–0.55). eCLIP binding and motif features added nothing in any setting. A gene-expression output layer gave the same picture (held-out r 0.015/0.036; cross-experiment ceiling 0.10–0.14). Inverse mapping of splicing shifts onto a dictionary of reproducible RBP responses [PENDING: H3 retrieval, TCGA mutant tumours, precancer cohorts, in-house validation]. The transferable signal in this compendium is confined to the core spliceosome class; sequence-specific regulators are not predictable from current binding data despite reproducible knockdown effects.

## Introduction (outline)

1. Perturbation prediction for unseen perturbations; recent benchmarks show deep models do not beat mean/linear baselines at the expression layer (Ahlmann-Eltze et al. 2025; Kernfeld et al. 2024 preprint; Csendes et al. 2025). Nobody has asked the question at the splicing layer with a held-out-RBP design.
2. The ENCODE RBP compendium (Van Nostrand et al. 2020) is the only resource with matched binding (eCLIP) and loss-of-function (knockdown RNA-seq) for hundreds of RBPs in the same cells. Prior integrations explain splicing changes of RBPs seen in training (KATMAP, McGurk et al. 2025; Raghav et al. 2026 preprint) or map binding positions (Van Nostrand et al. 2020 Genome Biology).
3. Three questions: (H1) does binding information transfer to unseen RBPs; (H2) what is the measurement ceiling of the compendium itself; (H3/H4) can measured responses be used in reverse to name the perturbed regulator in tumours and precancerous tissue.

## Results

### 1. A junction-level, coordinate-keyed knockdown splicing resource
- 876 knockdown/control pairs (HepG2 419: CRISPR 187, shRNA 232; K562 457: CRISPR 229, shRNA 228), rMATS-turbo on BAMs with GENCODE v29. 546,917 events keyed by coordinates (SE 395,073; MXE 115,740; A3SS 17,808; A5SS 11,780; RI 6,516). [10_event_universe/encode_full, STATUS 2026-11-07 01:35]
- Transcript-level PSI (SUPPA on RSEM TPM) is not usable as a target: split-half r 0.054/0.055, cross-technology r <= 0.08 even for EIF4A3/RBM25; junction-level dPSI of the same pairs reaches r 0.42–0.50. [04_h2_check, 08_junction_psi; STATUS 2026-11-04]
- Training universe: events covered (>= 10 reads both groups) in >= 50% of a cell line's runs: 46,476 (HepG2) and 50,190 (K562); 16.2 M and 19.0 M (event, run) rows.

### 2. Two artefacts that inflate apparent predictability
- Shared control: 102 control experiments are reused by 8.6 knockdowns on average (max 27). Unrelated RBPs sharing a control have dPSI r 0.49–0.51 (0.64–0.67 on significant events); unrelated RBPs with different controls 0.01–0.03. Fix: control-centring, dpsi_cc = dPSI minus the leave-one-out mean dPSI of the other knockdowns on the same control. After centring, shared-control pairs r −0.10/−0.08, true cross-technology signal preserved (RBM25 0.35 → 0.43). [12_baselines; STATUS 2026-11-07 11:00, 11:10]
- Control regression to the mean: rows called significant (FDR < 0.05, |dPSI| > 0.1) have mean |control deviation from the event's typical PSI| 0.086 vs 0.027 overall. The rule sign(−deviation) gives direction accuracy 0.830 (HepG2) / 0.794 (K562) on significant rows. Any model given the run-specific control PSI learns this shortcut; our v2 model's 0.69 was entirely of this kind (event-only model 0.815/0.790; shuffled-RBP model 0.691/0.671 = full model). [13_model/07_clean_target.py, logs/ctrl_artefact_check.txt; STATUS 2026-11-08 13:30]
- Clean target: events whose two knockdown replicates deviate from the control-group consensus in the same direction (each > 0.05) with |dpsi_cc| > 0.1 (4.1% of rows; 22–25% overlap with the FDR set). On this set the rule falls to 0.52.

### 3. Measurement ceilings
- Within-run split half (select |dPSI| > 0.1 in one replicate pair, test direction in the other): 0.538/0.541 over all runs; 0.62/0.64 for runs with >= 1,000 significant events; SF3B1 K562 0.80. [13_model/05_split_half_ceiling.py]
- Same RBP, independent experiment (shRNA vs CRISPR or two shRNA), on the clean set: 0.571/0.555 (0.636/0.616 on events clean in both); different-RBP zero point 0.494/0.496. [13_model/08_clean_flags_and_ceiling.py]
- About two thirds of RBPs (267/406 HepG2, 299/444 K562) have within-run direction agreement < 0.55: their knockdown splicing effects do not reproduce within the experiment.

### 4. Forward model and controls
- Architecture: event encoder (sequence, conservation, splice-site strength, 5-mer spectrum) x perturbation encoder (ESM2 + GenePT embedding of the RBP, PCA 32; motif density; eCLIP peak features in up/seg/down windows; assay) with a low-rank bilinear interaction; paralog-grouped 5-fold RBP hold-out (BLASTP >= 40% identity components; leakage report). Tiers A (all) / B (no eCLIP) / C (no eCLIP, no motif).
- v3 (clean target, no run-specific control PSI): RBP-mean direction accuracy 0.514/0.520; event-only 0.503/0.505; shuffled-RBP 0.512/0.500; v3 − shuffled +0.002 [−0.007, +0.011] / +0.020 [+0.007, +0.032]. A vs B vs C: |d| < 0.7 points. Best epochs mostly 0–2. [13_model/results_*_v3*.tsv, logs/v3_comparison.txt]
- Stratified by the RBP's own reproducibility: > 0.7 bin (11 / 20 RBPs) 0.678/0.694 vs shuffled 0.546/0.463 (paired +0.132 [+0.034, +0.233] / +0.231 [+0.109, +0.352]); < 0.55 bin difference 0.000/+0.009. Core spliceosome: SF3A1 K562 0.95 vs shuffled 0.55, SF3B3 0.94 vs 0.15, SF3B1 0.93 vs 0.77, U2AF2 HepG2 0.82 vs 0.33. Sequence-specific RBPs with reproducible knockdowns (PTBP1 0.64–0.69, SRSF1 0.75, HNRNPC 0.62–0.66) are not predicted (0.44–0.64, no better than shuffled). [13_model/stratified_results.tsv, fig_stratified_v3.png]
- Interpretation: the model recognises the core-spliceosome class from the protein/text embedding and predicts the class-typical large-scale response; binding-position features carry no transferable information in this resource. H1 negative.

### 7. Precancer cohorts (H4): negative
- GSE274628 fallopian tube, BRCA carrier (41) vs non-carrier (53), per-event OLS with batch and epithelial-score covariates (77,096 events tested; 3,875 at p < 0.05); GSE166044 normal breast, later-diagnosed (15) vs healthy (15), epithelial covariate (48,736 events; 1,825 at p < 0.05). Query = 500 smallest-p events with |beta| > 0.05, scored against all 478 dictionary RBPs.
- Top RBPs: SEC31A (r 0.26, fallopian, adjusted), PPP1R10 (0.28, breast, adjusted). Against 200 group-label permutations that repeat the whole pipeline, the observed top score is not unusual (p 0.29 and 0.12; unadjusted 0.14 and 0.47), and the number of RBPs with permutation p < 0.05 (30–33) equals the 5% expectation (23.9). No high-reproducibility RBP is significant after adjustment; SF3A3 and U2AF2 appear only in the unadjusted analyses and vanish with the epithelial covariate, i.e. they reflect cell-composition differences (the ENAH/CD44 epithelial–mesenchymal programme noted earlier). Permuted labels still yield >= 500 events at p < 0.05, so the group splicing differences are at noise level event-wise. [14_inverse/h4_summary.tsv, h4_*_ranking.tsv]
- Pre-registered second stage (sample-level activity scores = Pearson r of each sample's deviation from the cohort mean with every dictionary RBP; OLS activity ~ group + covariates; BH over RBPs; 1,000 label permutations; also clean-event dictionary, expression layer, and a premenopausal/batch-C subset). With batch, menopause and the epithelial score as covariates, the fallopian cohort gave family-wise p 0.006–0.031 and calls RRP9, EIF3D, EIF2S1 (consistent in sign across the four variants) and 66 expression-layer calls dominated by core spliceosome members (negative direction); the breast cohort gave 49 expression-layer calls (positive direction) and no splicing-layer call. The two cohorts disagreed in sign for every shared hit. After adjusting for all ten cell-composition scores plus age, every call disappeared (fallopian splicing 0/5 surviving, family-wise p 0.24; expression 0/66, p 0.27; breast expression 0/49, p 0.14). Activity scores correlate with composition scores up to |r| 0.6. The second-stage signals were composition differences refracted through the dictionary. [14_inverse/h4ext_*.tsv, h4rob_*.tsv]
- Is the composition adjustment an over-correction? Carriers do differ in composition (lower smooth-muscle, endothelial and immune scores; the ten scores explain 23% of carrier status), so we tested without regression: each carrier matched to its nearest non-carrier in composition space (41 pairs; 25 pairs within the premenopausal/batch-C stratum). RRP9, EIF3D, EIF2S1 and SUGP2 vanish (their activity tracks ciliated/endothelial content even within non-carriers, |r| up to 0.7). Two RBPs survive matching: SRSF3 (paired difference −0.020, sign-flip p 0.001; stratum p 0.026; high reproducibility) and CSNK1E (largest paired |t| of all 478, p 0.001; low reproducibility). SRSF3 activity is only weakly composition-related (|r| <= 0.27), but the signal is weak at the event level (53.5% of SRSF3's own clean events shift opposite to its knockdown vs 49.5% for random RBPs, p 0.05; r −0.08), has the opposite sign in the breast cohort (+0.011, p 0.09), and SRSF3 mRNA does not differ. We report it as suggestive and unreplicated, not as a positive. [14_inverse/h4adj_check.tsv, logs/srsf3_followup.txt]
- Reading: the retrieval method is sensitive to real perturbations (section 6, section 8 HeyA8), so this is a statement about the cohorts: the splicing shift of benign carrier fallopian tube and pre-diagnosis normal breast relative to controls is too weak and too composition-driven to be attributed to any specific RBP.

### 8. Post-freeze in-house validation
- HeyA8 LARP1 knockout vs wild type (3 vs 3; 73,602 events in the universe; 66 FDR-significant, so the query is p < 0.01 & |dPSI| > 0.1, top 500). Pre-registered comparators LARP4 and LARP7 rank 124 and 147 of 478 (not retrieved). The profile instead retrieves PCBP2 (rank 1, r 0.41, permutation p 0.005) and the U2 snRNP/SF3 complex: SF3B1 rank 3 (r 0.31, p 0.01), SF3A1 rank 6 (p 0.04), SF3B4 rank 11 (p 0.03); PRPF8 rank 5. Reading: the LARP1-knockout splicing shift resembles reduced core-spliceosome activity, a class-level signature consistent with LARP1 acting on translation rather than splicing, and with the module-level (not single-protein) splicing-factor association seen in the TMA proteomics (LARP1_Biomarker_paper). [14_inverse/heya8_ranking.tsv]
- TMA221/222 spatial proteomics (epithelial ROIs: 45 normal mucosa, 46 STIC, 37 tumour; 6,974 protein groups). The 30 RBPs with permutation p < 0.05 in the adjusted fallopian analysis (24 measured) show no consistent decrease along the progression axis: signed mean log2FC −0.08 (STIC vs normal) and −0.14 (tumour vs normal) vs +0.06 for 2,000 abundance- and detection-matched random sets (empirical p 0.94 / 0.97; 29–33% of proteins in the expected direction). Consistent with the negative H4 result. [14_inverse/tma_set_consistency.tsv]

## Discussion (points)
- Splicing-layer counterpart of the expression-layer benchmark conclusion: for unseen regulators, models match baselines except for one class.
- Reported accuracies on "significant events" with control PSI as input should be re-examined against the zero-parameter control-deviation rule.
- Two thirds of ENCODE knockdowns have no reproducible splicing effect; resource users should filter on reproducibility.
- What would be needed: position-resolved binding at higher resolution, matched-technology replicates, or perturbation-pretrained embeddings.

## Methods (draft)
Data. ENCODE shRNA and CRISPR knockdown RNA-seq (HepG2, K562) BAMs and IDR-merged eCLIP narrowPeaks (release 2026-11 metadata, 01_encode_metadata). rMATS-turbo (GENCODE v29, read length per experiment) on each knockdown/control pair; events keyed by (type, chr, strand, exon coordinates). Coverage >= 10 reads per group.
Control-centring. For run i with control c, dpsi_cc,i(e) = dPSI_i(e) − mean_{j≠i, control(j)=c} dPSI_j(e); runs whose control is used by no other knockdown are dropped (10 HepG2, 11 K562).
Clean target. Replicate-consistent deviation from the control-group consensus (defined above).
Ceilings. Within-run split half; same-RBP independent runs; different-RBP zero point (13_model/05, 08).
Features. Event: type, segment length/GC, intron lengths, MaxEntScan donor/acceptor, phastCons100way (segment, flanking 100 nt), 5-mer spectra of upstream 300 nt / segment / downstream 300 nt. RBP: ESM2-650M mean embedding, GenePT text embedding, motif 5-mer vectors (ATtRACT/RBNS), eCLIP max signalValue and −log10 p in the three windows.
Splits. Paralog-grouped 5-fold RBP hold-out by all-vs-all BLASTP (>= 40% identity, alignment >= 30% of the shorter protein; connected components), inner validation fold for model selection.
Model. As in 13_model/01_train.py; Huber loss (δ 0.1) with weight 100 on target rows, AdamW 3e-4, cosine decay, 20 epochs, batch 2048, model selection by n_sig-weighted RBP-mean r on the validation fold. Controls: rbp_mode none (event-only), shuffle (fixed RBP permutation).
Expression layer. 13_model/10–12; genes mean TPM >= 1; target = KD mean log2(TPM+1) − leave-one-out consensus of other knockdowns on the same control; sig = replicate-consistent deviation > 0.2 and |Y| > 0.5; knocked-down gene excluded.
Inverse mapping. 14_inverse/retrieval.py: Pearson r between a query dPSI vector (selected events) and each dictionary RBP's mean dpsi_cc over shared events (>= 50); ranks; permutation nulls as described per analysis. TCGA PSI from Snaptron (tcgav2) exact junction queries; mutations from cBioPortal PanCanAtlas studies.
Statistics. RBP-level paired bootstrap (2,000 resamples) for differences; empirical permutation p values.
